# Third-party assets and licences

Everything below is CC0 / public domain. Nothing from Rockstar Games is used.

## Textures (assets/tex) and sky reflection map (assets/sky_env.jpg)
From Poly Haven, https://polyhaven.com, CC0. Thank you to the artists.
- Asphalt 01 (roads), Concrete Pavement (sidewalks), Blue Plaster Weathered (building walls, desaturated), Brick Wall 005 (building walls), Clay Roof Tiles (roofs)
- Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky) HDRI, tone-mapped to a 256x128 JPG for soft reflections on roads and water
- Resized to 512 px and recompressed for the web. Per-file list in assets/tex/LICENSES.md

## 3D models (assets/props.bin, assets/props.json)
From kayquer/kit-cidade-lowpoly, https://github.com/kayquer/kit-cidade-lowpoly, CC0 1.0, by Kayque Rotondo and Claudia Dias Roxinol.
39 models: houses, apartment blocks, offices, shops, bank, hospital, school, police and fire stations, vehicles, hydrant, bench, bin, street lamp, stop sign, bus stop and trees.
Packed by tools/pack_props.py (colours baked to vertices, positions quantised to 1/1024 unit).

## Map
Aerial imagery by Ian Albert (CC-BY-SA) via SanMap, https://github.com/jamiemansfield/SanMap (MIT). Road network traced from the SanMap line map by tools in this repo.

## Not used
lowpoly.download (licence forbids redistributing the models), Meshy and Tripo (sign-up required), Jaron's asset pack (ruined industrial theme, does not fit a living city).
